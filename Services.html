SERVICE 01

CERTIFICATES & DOCUMENTS

Barangay Clearance

A barangay clearance may be requested for employment, business-related transactions, or other purposes that require local barangay certification. The exact requirements depend on the purpose of your request and the applicable local procedures.

Documents to Prepare or Confirm

  • Valid government-issued identification
  • Proof of residence, if required
  • Completed application form, if required
  • Details about the purpose of the clearance
  • Applicable fee, if any
!
Before applying

Ask the barangay office which identification documents are accepted and whether additional requirements apply to your specific purpose.

View Application Steps →

SERVICE 02

COMMUNITY SUPPORT

Certificate of Indigency

A certificate of indigency may be requested by residents who need documentation of their financial circumstances for a purpose accepted by the receiving institution or agency. Issuance may involve verification by the barangay.

Documents to Prepare or Confirm

  • Valid identification
  • Proof of residence, if required
  • Purpose of the requested certificate
  • Supporting documents, if requested
  • Additional information needed for verification
!
Important reminder

Requirements and eligibility depend on the applicable local procedures and the purpose for which the certificate will be used.

View Application Steps →

SERVICE 03

RESIDENCY DOCUMENTATION

Certificate of Residency

A certificate of residency may be used to confirm that an individual lives within Barangay Biringan. The barangay office determines the acceptable evidence of residence and the verification process.

Documents to Prepare or Confirm

  • Valid identification
  • Proof of address or residence, if required
  • Completed request form, if required
  • Additional supporting documents, if requested
!
Proof of residence

Ask the office which documents are accepted to establish your current residential address.

SERVICE 04

COMMUNITY PROGRAMS

Community Assistance

Residents may contact the barangay office to inquire about community programs, referrals, local initiatives, and other forms of assistance. Availability and eligibility depend on the programs currently offered and the requirements of the responsible office.

How to Make an Inquiry

  • Prepare identification, if needed
  • Explain the type of assistance you are seeking
  • Bring supporting documents, if available
  • Ask about eligibility and application procedures
  • Confirm program schedules and next steps
!
Program availability

Contact the barangay directly for confirmed program schedules, eligibility rules, application deadlines, and available support.

Helpful information

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about barangay services and application procedures.

Where can I request a barangay certificate?

Contact or visit the barangay office to confirm where requests are accepted, which documents you need, and the procedure for your chosen certificate.

How much does a barangay certificate cost?

Fees depend on the service and applicable local rules. Ask the barangay office for the current official fee schedule. Do not rely on unverified online fee information.

How long does processing take?

Processing time may vary depending on the requested document and any verification needed. Confirm the expected timeframe when submitting your request.

Can I apply for services online?

You can fill out the application form on this page. Your request still needs to be confirmed by the barangay office, and you must bring a valid ID when you collect your document. Confirm the available application channels with the barangay office.

What should I bring when visiting the barangay office?

Bring valid identification and any supporting documents requested for your specific service. Confirm the complete list of requirements before travelling to the office.

Where can I find the barangay and SK officials?

Visit the Officials page to see the barangay officials and Sangguniang Kabataan representatives listed on the website.
